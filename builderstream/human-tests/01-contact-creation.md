# Phase 1–2: Contact Creation

**Time required:** ~5 minutes
**Where you start:** Anywhere — you'll navigate to Sales → CRM
**Goal:** Add Mike Johnson as a contact so he can be assigned to a lead and later a project.

---

## Why This Step Matters

Every person you do business with — homeowners, GCs, property managers — lives in the Contacts list. A contact must exist before you can open a lead. Getting the contact right sets up clean data for everything that follows.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1 — Navigate to CRM

1. In the left sidebar, click **Sales**.
2. The page will load the CRM section. You should see a sub-navigation bar at the top with **CRM** and **Estimating**.
3. Make sure **CRM** is selected (it should be the default).

**✅ You should see:** Two tabs labeled **leads** and **contacts**, plus four KPI cards at the top (Total Leads, Hot Leads, Pipeline Value, Contacts).

---

### Step 2 — Open the Contacts Tab

1. Click the **contacts** tab (just below the KPI cards).

**✅ You should see:** A table with columns: Name, Email, Phone, Company, Title, Score. It may be empty or have existing contacts.

---

### Step 3 — Open the New Contact Form

1. Click the **+ New Contact** button (top-right of the contacts table).

**✅ You should see:** A modal popup titled **"New Contact"** with a form.

---

### Step 4 — Fill In the Contact Details

Enter these values exactly:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| First Name | Mike |
| Last Name | Johnson |
| Email | mike.johnson@testclient.com |
| Phone | (555) 210-4400 |
| Mobile | (leave blank) |
| Company | Johnson Renovations |
| Job Title | Owner |
| Notes | Referred by neighbor. Interested in master bath remodel at 1204 Oak St. |

> **Note:** First Name and Last Name are required (marked with *). Email and Phone are optional but filling them in is good practice.

---

### Step 5 — Save the Contact

1. Click the **Create Contact** button at the bottom of the modal.

**✅ You should see:** The modal closes. Mike Johnson appears in the contacts table with his name, email, phone, company, and a lead score (may show 0 or a calculated number).

---

## Verification Checklist

After saving, confirm the following:

- [ ] Mike Johnson appears in the contacts list
- [ ] His email shows as `mike.johnson@testclient.com`
- [ ] Company shows as `Johnson Renovations`
- [ ] The **Contacts** KPI card count increased by 1

---

## Common Issues

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| "Create Contact" button is greyed out | First Name or Last Name is missing — both are required |
| Modal closes but contact doesn't appear | Click the contacts tab again to refresh the list |
| Duplicate name warning | Someone with that name already exists — check the list first |
