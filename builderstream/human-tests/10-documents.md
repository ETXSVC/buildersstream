# Phase 12: Documents — RFIs, Submittals & Photos

**Time required:** ~10 minutes
**Where you start:** Projects → Documents (sub-nav)
**Prerequisite:** Project is In Progress
**Goal:** Submit an RFI, track a submittal, and upload photos to the project photo log.

---

## Why This Step Matters

The document trail is your protection. An RFI (Request for Information) creates a written record when field conditions don't match the plans. Submittals ensure that the materials and products you're installing are pre-approved before they go in. The photo log documents progress and condition for insurance, warranty, and client satisfaction.

---

## Navigation

1. In the left sidebar, click **Projects**.
2. In the sub-navigation bar at the top, click **Documents**.

**✅ You should see:** Four tabs: **Documents**, **RFIs**, **Submittals**, **Photos** and four KPI cards: Documents, Open RFIs, Pending Submittals, Photos.

---

## Part A — Submit an RFI

### Scenario

During tile installation, the field crew discovers the floor is 1.5" lower than the plan shows due to an existing subfloor dip. They need direction on whether to use a self-leveling compound (adds $400) or adjust the tile pattern.

### Step 1 — Go to RFIs Tab

1. Click the **RFIs** tab.

**✅ You should see:** A table with columns: RFI #, Project, Title, Submitted By, Due Date, Status.

### Step 2 — Create a New RFI

1. Click **+ New RFI**.

**✅ You should see:** A modal titled **"New RFI"**.

### Step 3 — Fill In the RFI

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Project * | Master Bath Remodel – Johnson |
| Subject * | Floor elevation discrepancy — self-leveling compound required? |
| Status | Open |
| Question * | During floor tile layout, we found a 1.5" elevation drop across the northwest corner of the bathroom floor (approx 12 sq ft area). Plans show a flat floor. Recommend: Option A — apply self-leveling compound (~$400 materials + labor add) to achieve flat surface before tile. Option B — adjust tile layout to minimize visibility of slope. Please advise how to proceed and confirm if Option A is approved as a cost addition. |
| Answer | (leave blank — this is filled in when the answer comes back) |
| Due Date | (three days from today — field work is waiting on this answer) |

### Step 4 — Save

1. Click **Create RFI**.

**✅ Result:** RFI appears in the table with an auto-generated RFI # (e.g., RFI-001), status "Open," and the due date.

### Step 5 — Answer the RFI (Simulating a Response)

1. Hover over the RFI row → click **Edit**.
2. Fill in:

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Answered |
| Answer | Approved Option A. Apply self-leveling compound to NW corner. Submit updated change order for the $400 materials and labor addition. Proceed immediately — do not hold up tile schedule. |

3. Click **Save Changes**.

**✅ Result:** RFI status changes to "Answered." **Open RFIs** KPI card decreases by 1.

## Part B — Track a Submittal

### Scenario

Before installing the shower valve and trim, you need owner/architect approval of the specific fixture model.

### Step 1 — Go to Submittals Tab

1. Click the **Submittals** tab.

**✅ You should see:** A table with columns: #, Project, Title, Spec Section, Submitted, Required By, Status.

### Step 2 — Create a New Submittal

1. Click **+ New Submittal**.

**✅ You should see:** A modal titled **"New Submittal"**.

### Step 3 — Fill In the Submittal

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Project | Master Bath Remodel – Johnson |
| Title * | Shower Valve & Trim — Kohler Rite-Temp K-P304-K-CP |
| Spec Section | 22 40 00 – Plumbing Fixtures |
| Status | Submitted |
| Submitted Date | (today) |
| Required By | (in 5 days — need approval before installation begins) |
| Review Notes | Proposing Kohler Rite-Temp pressure-balancing valve with Kohler Loure trim in Polished Chrome (K-T14490-4-CP). Submittal package includes product data sheet and installation instructions. |

### Step 4 — Save

1. Click **Create Submittal**.

**✅ Result:** Submittal appears in the table with status "Submitted."

### Step 5 — Mark as Approved

After the design team reviews:

1. Hover → Edit.
2. Change Status to **Approved**.
3. Add Review Notes: "Approved as submitted. Proceed with Kohler Rite-Temp K-P304-K-CP."
4. Save.

**✅ Result:** Submittal shows "Approved." **Pending Submittals** KPI card decreases.

## Part C — View the Photo Log

### Step 1 — Go to Photos Tab

1. Click the **Photos** tab.

**✅ You should see:** A photo grid or table showing uploaded project photos.

### Step 2 — Note the Upload Capability

The photo log stores before/during/after photos for the project. Photos can typically be uploaded via the field ops mobile view or the documents page.

> **For this test:** Note how many photos are in the system. In a live project you would upload photos of:
> - Pre-demo condition
> - Demo complete
> - Rough plumbing and electrical
> - Tile in progress
> - Completed work before final inspection

---

## Common Issues

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Answer field not visible on RFI | The Answer field appears when status is set to "Answered" — change status first |
| Submittal spec section not a dropdown | Spec Section is a free-text field — type the spec number directly |
| Photos tab shows no upload button | Photo upload may be done through the Field Ops mobile interface |
