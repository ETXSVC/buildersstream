# Phase 17: Teams & Collaboration Archive/Restore

**Time required:** ~15 minutes
**Where you start:** Company section
**Prerequisite:** Logged in as admin@builderstream.com
**Goal:** Create a team, add members, assign the team to a project, and archive/restore a chat channel.

---

## Why This Step Matters

Teams let you group your workforce into named crews (e.g. "Framing Crew", "Electrical Team") and assign them directly to projects. Both W-2 employees and 1099 contractors can be team members — no platform login required. Archiving a channel keeps your Team Messaging sidebar clean without losing history.

---

## Part A — Create a Team

### Step 1 — Go to Company

1. In the left sidebar, click **Company**.
2. You should see KPI cards at the top: Total Employees, Total Contractors, Total Workforce, Team Members, Teams.

### Step 2 — Switch to the Teams Tab

1. In the tab bar below the KPI cards, click **Teams**.
2. You will see a **+ New Team** button and a list of existing teams (may be empty).

### Step 3 — Create a New Team

1. Click **+ New Team**.
2. In the Name field, type: `Test Crew`
3. In the Description field, type: `Remodel specialists`
4. Click **Create**.

**Expected:** The new team "Test Crew" appears in the list with "0 members."

---

## Part B — Add Members to the Team

### Step 4 — Expand the Team

1. Click the **▶ chevron** or the team row to expand it.
2. You should see the team's member list (empty) and an **Add Member** section.

### Step 5 — Add a Member

1. In the **Select person** dropdown, choose any employee or contractor from the list.
   - Both W-2 employees and 1099 contractors appear here.
2. In the **Role** dropdown, select **Team Lead**.
3. Click **Add**.

**Expected:** The person appears in the member list with their name, trade, and role "Team Lead."

### Step 6 — Change a Member's Role

1. Next to the member you just added, find the role dropdown showing "Team Lead".
2. Change it to **Member**.

**Expected:** The role updates immediately — no save button required.

### Step 7 — Remove a Member

1. Click the **×** button next to the member.

**Expected:** The member disappears from the list. The team shows "0 members."

Re-add the member with role **Team Lead** before continuing.

---

## Part C — Check the Team Members Tab

### Step 8 — View Combined Workforce

1. Click the **Team Members** tab in the tab bar.
2. This list shows **all employees and contractors combined** (both W-2 and 1099).

**Expected:** You can see both employee types in one table. This is different from the Employees or Contractors tabs which show each type separately.

---

## Part D — Assign a Team to a Project

### Step 9 — Go to Projects

1. In the left sidebar, click **Projects**.

### Step 10 — Edit a Project

1. Hover over any existing project card.
2. Click the **pencil (edit)** icon that appears.

### Step 11 — Assign the Team

1. In the edit modal, find the **Assigned Team** field (a dropdown).
2. Select **Test Crew** from the list.
3. Click **Save**.

**Expected:** The project card now shows "Test Crew" below the project manager name.

---

## Part E — Archive and Restore a Channel

### Step 12 — Go to Team Messaging

1. In the left sidebar, click **Team Messaging**.

### Step 13 — Archive a Channel

1. In the sidebar, hover over any channel under **Channels** (e.g. "general").
2. A **•••** button appears on the right side of the channel row.
3. Click **•••**.
4. Click **Archive**.

**Expected:** The channel immediately disappears from the active Channels list and moves to the **Archived** section at the bottom of the sidebar.

### Step 14 — View Archived Channels

1. Scroll to the bottom of the Team Messaging sidebar.
2. Click **Archived** to expand the section.

**Expected:** The channel you archived in Step 13 appears in the list.

### Step 15 — Restore the Channel

1. Hover over the archived channel.
2. A **•••** button appears.
3. Click **•••** → **Restore**.

**Expected:** The channel moves back to the active Channels list at the top of the sidebar.

---

## Part F — Archive a Direct Message (Close)

### Step 16 — Close a DM

1. Hover over any entry under **Direct Messages** in the sidebar.
2. Click **•••** → **Close**.

**Expected:** The DM disappears from the Direct Messages section and moves to **Archived**.

---

## Verification Checklist

Before marking this phase complete, confirm:

- [ ] "Test Crew" team was created successfully
- [ ] At least one employee/contractor was added as Team Lead
- [ ] Team Members tab shows both W-2 and 1099 workers combined
- [ ] "Test Crew" was assigned to a project and appears on the project card
- [ ] Archiving a channel removes it from the active list
- [ ] Archived section is visible and expandable at sidebar bottom

## Common Issues

| Problem | Likely Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No employees in Add Member dropdown | No employees created yet | Go to Company → Employees tab → Add Employee first |
| Team name already exists error | Name must be unique per org | Use a different name |
| Assigned Team dropdown is empty | No teams created yet | Complete Part A first |
| ••• button not visible | Hover directly over the channel row | Move mouse closer to the channel name |
| Archived section not visible | No archived channels yet | Archive a channel first (Step 13) |
