# Phase 5–6: Estimating & Proposals

**Time required:** ~15 minutes
**Where you start:** Sales → Estimating
**Prerequisite:** Mike Johnson lead exists in "Site Visit" stage
**Goal:** Build a detailed estimate, then generate and send a proposal to the client.

---

## Why This Step Matters

The estimate is the financial backbone of the project. A well-built estimate with clear line items protects you from scope creep and gives the client a transparent view of costs. The proposal turns the estimate into a client-facing document they can review and sign off on.

---

## Part A — Create the Estimate

### Step 1 — Navigate to Estimating

1. In the left sidebar, click **Sales**.
2. In the sub-navigation bar, click **Estimating** (next to CRM).

**✅ You should see:** Two tabs: **estimates** and **proposals**, plus KPI cards: Total Estimates, Approved, Total Est. Value, Win Rate.

### Step 2 — Open the New Estimate Form

1. Make sure you're on the **estimates** tab.
2. Click **+ New Estimate** (top-right).

**✅ You should see:** A modal titled **"New Estimate"** with a form.

### Step 3 — Fill in Estimate Header

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Estimate Name * | Master Bath Remodel – Johnson Renovations |
| Status | Draft |
| Project | (leave blank for now — we haven't created the project yet) |
| Tax Rate (%) | 8.25 |
| Valid Until | (today + 30 days) |
| Notes | Estimate for master bathroom remodel at 1204 Oak Street, Austin TX. Scope includes demolition, framing, tile, plumbing rough-in/finish, vanity installation, shower system. |

### Step 4 — Save the Estimate Header

1. Click **Create Estimate**.

**✅ You should see:** The modal closes. The estimate appears in the estimates table with status "Draft" and a generated estimate number (e.g., EST-2026-001).

## Part B — Add Line Items to the Estimate

> **Note:** Line items are added after the estimate is created, by editing it. Hover over the estimate row and click the **Edit** icon.

After clicking Edit you should see the estimate form. Look for a **Line Items** or **Sections** area below the header fields where you can add cost breakdown items.

Add these line items:

| Description | Quantity | Unit | Unit Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demolition & disposal | 1 | lump sum | 2,500 |
| Framing & rough carpentry | 1 | lump sum | 3,000 |
| Plumbing rough-in | 1 | lump sum | 4,500 |
| Plumbing fixtures & finish | 1 | lump sum | 6,000 |
| Tile — floor & shower walls (300 sq ft) | 300 | sq ft | 18 |
| Tile installation labor | 300 | sq ft | 12 |
| Double vanity (owner-supplied) | 1 | ea | 0 |
| Vanity installation | 1 | lump sum | 800 |
| Electrical (GFCIs, exhaust fan) | 1 | lump sum | 1,200 |
| Paint — walls & ceiling | 1 | lump sum | 900 |
| Miscellaneous & allowances | 1 | lump sum | 2,500 |

**Expected subtotal before tax:** ~$35,300
**With 8.25% tax on materials:** ~$38,000–$39,000

Click **Save Changes** after entering all line items.

---

## Part C — Change Status to "In Review"

Once the estimate looks complete:

1. Hover over the estimate row → click **Edit**.
2. Change **Status** to **In Review**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

**✅ Result:** Estimate status now shows "In Review."

---

## Part D — Approve the Estimate

After internal review (or just for this test):

1. Hover over the estimate row.
2. Look for the **Approve** action button (appears on hover).
3. Click **Approve**.

**✅ Result:** Status changes to "Approved." The **Approved** KPI card count increases.

---

## Part E — Generate a Proposal

A proposal is a client-facing version of the estimate.

### Step 1 — Generate the Proposal

1. Hover over the approved estimate row.
2. Click the **Proposal** action button (this converts the estimate into a proposal).

**✅ You should see:** A modal titled **"Generate Proposal — [Estimate Number]"**.

### Step 2 — Fill in Proposal Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Client * | Mike Johnson (start typing, select from dropdown) |
| Valid Until | (today + 14 days) |

### Step 3 — Generate

1. Click **Generate Proposal** (or the equivalent confirm button).

**✅ Result:** You're redirected to (or can switch to) the **proposals** tab. The new proposal appears with status "Draft."

---

## Part F — Send the Proposal

### Step 1 — Go to Proposals Tab

1. Click the **proposals** tab.
2. Find the proposal for Mike Johnson.

### Step 2 — Send It

1. Hover over the proposal row → click **Send**.

**✅ You should see:** A modal titled **"Send Proposal — [Proposal #]"**.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Recipient Email | mike.johnson@testclient.com |

### Step 3 — Confirm Send

1. Click **Send Proposal**.

**✅ Result:** Proposal status changes to "Sent" and the **Sent** column updates. In a real scenario, Mike would receive an email with a link to review and sign.

## Common Issues

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No line items section visible | The estimate header must be saved first — edit the estimate again |
| Client dropdown empty in proposal generation | Mike Johnson contact must exist in the CRM |
| Proposal status stays "Draft" after send | Click Mark Sent action instead of Send if the email send fails |
