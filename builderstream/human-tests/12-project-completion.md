# Phase 14: Project Completion

**Time required:** ~10 minutes
**Where you start:** Projects section
**Prerequisite:** Project tasks complete, final inspection scheduled
**Goal:** Complete the final inspection, advance the project status to completion, and close out all open items.

---

## Why This Step Matters

Project closeout is the formal hand-off from contractor to client. It ensures every punch-list item is resolved, the final inspection is on record, and the project is officially marked complete in the system — which triggers the final billing cycle and stops the health score clock.

---

## Part A — Complete the Final Inspection

### Step 1 — Go to Quality & Safety

1. In the left sidebar, click **Operations**.
2. In the sub-nav, click **Quality & Safety**.
3. Click the **Inspections** tab.

### Step 2 — Find the Final Inspection

Locate the "Final" type inspection you created in Phase 11 (scheduled for the project completion date).

### Step 3 — Mark It Complete

1. Hover over the final inspection row → click **Edit**.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Passed |
| Score | 97 |
| Notes | Final walkthrough passed. All tile work complete and grouted. Shower valve functions correctly. Vanity and fixtures installed and operational. Paint complete. All caulking finished. Minor punch item: touch-up paint near window trim — completed same day. Client extremely satisfied. |

2. Click **Save Changes**.

**✅ Result:** Final inspection shows "Passed" with score 97. Pass rate improves.

---

## Part B — Close Out Open Tasks

### Step 1 — Go to Scheduling

1. In the left sidebar, click **Projects**.
2. In sub-nav, click **Scheduling** → **tasks** tab.

### Step 2 — Mark All Tasks Complete

For each task in the Master Bath Remodel project:

1. Hover over the task → Edit.
2. Set **Status** to **Completed**.
3. Set **% Complete** to **100**.
4. Save.

Repeat for all remaining tasks. The **Completed** KPI card on the Scheduling page should increase.

> **Quick tip:** Start with the milestone task "Final Inspection" and work backwards.

---

## Part C — Update Project Status to Final Stages

Progress through the remaining statuses:

### Step 1 — Go to Projects

1. Click **Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Find Master Bath Remodel – Johnson.

### Step 2 — Advance to "Milestones" Status

1. Hover → Edit → change Status to **Milestones** → Save.

### Step 3 — Advance to "Finish Project"

1. Hover → Edit → change Status to **Finish Project** → Save.

### Step 4 — Advance to "Billing"

1. Hover → Edit → change Status to **Billing** → Save.

> This status signals that final billing should now be prepared. The team knows no more field work is happening — only invoicing and client payment remain.

**✅ Result:** Project status shows "Billing."

---

## Part D — Review Project Health Score

Before closing:

1. Find the project in the list.
2. Look at the health score bar and badge.
3. With tasks complete, budget on track, and no overdue items, the score should be **Green / 85–100**.

> If the score is red or yellow, the most common reasons are:
> - Outstanding unpaid invoices (financial variance)
> - Tasks marked as overdue but not yet completed
> - Budget overage from expenses exceeding estimate

---

## Part E — Final Status: "Paid / Complete"

After the final invoice is paid (covered in Phase 15), advance the project to fully closed:

1. Hover → Edit → change Status to **Paid / Complete** → Save.

**✅ Result:** Project status shows "Paid / Complete." It will no longer appear in the **Active** KPI card filter on the projects page.

---

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] Final inspection shows "Passed" with score 97
- [ ] All project tasks show "Completed" with 100% progress
- [ ] Project status is at least "Billing" (or "Paid / Complete" if you're jumping ahead)
- [ ] No overdue tasks in Scheduling for this project
- [ ] **Completed** KPI card on Scheduling shows the completed task count
- [ ] Project health score is Green

---

## Common Issues

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Can't advance to "Paid / Complete" | The system may require at least one paid invoice — complete Phase 15 first |
| Health score still red after completing tasks | Score recalculates hourly — wait a few minutes or check if invoices are overdue |
| Tasks show as overdue even after marking complete | Check the % Complete field — it must be set to 100, not just the status |
