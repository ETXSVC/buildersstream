# Phase 3: Lead Creation

**Time required:** ~5 minutes
**Where you start:** Sales → CRM → leads tab
**Prerequisite:** Mike Johnson contact exists (see `01-contact-creation.md`)
**Goal:** Open a lead for the master bath remodel and place it in the sales pipeline.

---

## Why This Step Matters

A Lead is a sales opportunity tied to a contact. It lives on the pipeline board and gets moved through stages as the deal progresses. Every opportunity — whether it becomes a project or not — should be tracked as a lead so you have a record of your sales activity, conversion rate, and pipeline value.

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1 — Go to the Leads Tab

1. In the left sidebar, click **Sales**.
2. Click the **leads** tab (left of the two tabs below the KPI cards).

**✅ You should see:** A table with columns: Contact, Stage, Project Type, Value, Urgency, Follow-up.

---

### Step 2 — Open the New Lead Form

1. Click the **+ New Lead** button (top-right of the leads table).

**✅ You should see:** A modal titled **"New Lead"** with a form.

---

### Step 3 — Fill In the Lead Details

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Contact * | Mike Johnson (start typing, select from dropdown) |
| Pipeline Stage * | New Lead |
| Project Type | Kitchen & Bath |
| Estimated Value ($) | 45000 |
| Urgency | Warm – 1–3 Months |
| Estimated Start | (set to 3 weeks from today) |
| Next Follow-up | (set to 3 days from today) |
| Description | Master bathroom remodel at 1204 Oak Street, Austin TX. Client wants walk-in shower, new tile, double vanity. Referred by neighbor. |

> **Urgency options explained:**
> - **Hot – Immediate:** Client wants to start within days/weeks
> - **Warm – 1–3 Months:** Active interest, not yet urgent
> - **Cold – 3+ Months:** Early inquiry, no firm timeline

---

### Step 4 — Save the Lead

1. Click the **Create Lead** button.

**✅ You should see:** The modal closes. The new lead appears in the leads table showing Mike Johnson's name, the "New Lead" stage, "Kitchen & Bath" type, $45,000 value, and "Warm" urgency.

---

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] Lead appears in the leads list with Mike Johnson as contact
- [ ] Stage shows "New Lead"
- [ ] Estimated value shows $45,000
- [ ] Urgency shows "Warm"
- [ ] Next Follow-up date is set

- [ ] **Pipeline Value** KPI card shows the added $45,000

## Clicking a KPI Card

Try clicking the **Total Leads** KPI card at the top of the CRM page. It should scroll you down to the leads list with all leads visible. Click **Hot Leads** and it filters to show only hot-urgency leads.

---

## Common Issues

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Contact field shows no results | The contact wasn't saved — go back to 01-contact-creation.md |
| Stage dropdown is empty | Pipeline stages need to be set up — check Settings if needed |
| Lead doesn't appear after saving | Click the **leads** tab to reload the list |
